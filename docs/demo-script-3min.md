# IdeateX — 3-Minute Demo Script

**Before you record:**
- Have a **completed run open in a second tab** (or `/dev/fixture`) — a real run won't finish inside the recording, so you'll launch live, show the run in flight, then cut to the finished one.
- Seed the persona library (`pnpm seed-personas`) so `/personas` looks full.
- Practice pace: narration below is ~440 words ≈ 3 minutes at a relaxed pace.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 — The hook (home page)

**[SCREEN: home page]**

> "Every marketing team launches campaigns on a guess. IdeateX replaces the guess with a race: before you spend a dollar, we pit three campaign strategies against each other inside a synthetic population — hundreds of AI personas that react, argue, and pick a winner. Let me show you a full run."

## 0:20 – 0:45 — The population (`/personas`)

**[SCREEN: navigate to /personas, scroll the library, open one persona card]**

> "It starts with the population. This library holds over a hundred personas — each with real demographics, psychographics, and a point of view. Here's one — you can see exactly who they are and what they care about. You can also generate a custom pool from a single prompt, so the audience actually matches *your* market."

## 0:45 – 1:20 — Launching a run (new run form)

**[SCREEN: new run form. Type product name, description, audience, objective. Point at tiers, toggles, live cost estimate. Click "Launch run"]**

> "Now the run. I describe my product, who it's for, and what I want the campaign to achieve. I pick a tier — Quick, Standard, or Deep — which sets how big a population we sweep. I can turn on live web grounding so the advisors check real-world context, and notice the cost estimate updating live — a full run costs cents, not a focus-group budget. Launch."

## 1:20 – 2:05 — The run, live (run page)

**[SCREEN: live run page. Trace the stage progress bar, then the agent graph fanning out, then the race panel]**

> "Here's where it gets fun. First, planners cast personas from the library into one shared audience with a follow graph — a miniature social network. Then three strategists each pitch a different campaign strategy. And now the race: every strategy gets dropped into the same simulated feed, tick by tick, and we watch personas react, share, and push back — you can click any agent to read its actual reasoning. Then an advisory panel — three lenses plus a moderator — debates the results and picks the winner."

**[Cut to the completed run tab around 2:05]**

## 2:05 – 2:45 — The results report

**[SCREEN: results page. Scroll: Verdict → The strategy race → Focus group → In their own words → The campaign package]**

> "And this is the deliverable. A verdict with the winning strategy and why it won. The race data behind it. Then we take the winner and run a deep swarm — a focus group of personas stress-testing it, in their own words — real quotes, real objections. And it ends with a campaign package: drafted content, an objection ledger, even a PR pre-mortem. Not a score — a plan."

## 2:45 – 3:00 — Close

**[SCREEN: rest on the Verdict card]**

> "Ten minutes, a few cents, and you know which campaign wins *before* the world sees it. That's IdeateX — race your ideas before you launch them."

---

**Fallback:** if the live run stalls on camera, stay on the agent graph for one beat, say "while this cooks, here's one that just finished," and cut — the script already assumes the cut at 2:05.
